  100% CLIENT-SIDE PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Effective Immediately

1. Core Privacy Philosophy: Zero Server Uploads

At DevOpsVaultX (accessible from www.devopsvaultx.com), your privacy is our foundational architectural principle. Unlike conventional web services, our tools (including Merge PDF, Image to PDF, Password Generator, QR Generator, and converters) execute 100% client-side inside your web browser using modern JavaScript, HTML5 APIs, and WebAssembly.

Your files, photos, passwords, and sensitive documents never leave your local computer or phone. They are never uploaded, stored, or processed on our backend servers.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • No personal identification data (no sign-up, no passwords, no email collection).
  • No document contents, PDF text, images, or files.
  • No clipboard or converted text data.

3. Third-Party CDNs & Zero-Cookie Policy

We deliver static visual assets (such as Font Awesome icons) via certified zero-cookie Content Delivery Networks (jsDelivr). We do not set any third-party tracking cookies on your device.

4. Aggregated Anonymous Analytics

We use Google Analytics (GA4) with IP anonymization to understand high-level traffic metrics (such as aggregate page views and browser types) to ensure server stability. GA4 does not track individual user identity or any data processed within the tools.

5. Contact & Questions

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact Page or join our community on GitHub and YouTube.
